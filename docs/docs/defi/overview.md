# Experimental Labs

This section contains non-production research. It is intentionally separated from the wallet, hardware and trade product.

Potential experiments include:

- Optional Soroban anchoring of settlement proofs
- Community governance of pricing coefficients
- Liquidity mechanisms for future energy markets
- Flash lending research
- Carbon accounting proofs

Stelltron does **not** claim a live APY, earned yield, governance vote or production DeFi pool. Any future financial mechanism requires security review, economic validation and applicable regulatory work before it can move into the product.
