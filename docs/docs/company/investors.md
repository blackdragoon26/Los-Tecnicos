## Thesis

Energy scarcity, distributed solar and household storage create a need for infrastructure below the utility layer. Stelltron gives private individuals a way to measure, advertise, route and settle surplus stored energy through local hardware.

## Decentralization model

Decentralization is physical and operational first: households own Power Kits, nearby Raspberry Pi gateways coordinate local transfers, and the backend keeps a transparent LT ledger. Soroban can anchor proofs without blocking local settlement.

## Current evidence

- Physical 5V/18650 prototype with ESP32 control and relay-switched supply/receive paths
- Raspberry Pi edge controller and telemetry protocol
- Backend wallet, escrow, pricing and trade lifecycle
- Two-person browser demo with separate identities
- 50-home Delhi NCR digital twin driven by weather and time
- [Stellar APAC Winner](https://stellar.org/)

The digital twin is a projection tool. It is not represented as customer traction.

## Roadmap

1. Harden metering and electrical safety for a household pilot.
2. Validate transfer efficiency and thermal behavior with calibrated instruments.
3. Pilot multiple Power Kits behind one local gateway.
4. Connect nearby gateways into a resilient community mesh.
5. Add optional settlement proofs and compliant funding integrations.
